FnF Easter egg assets

The minigame uses the chart and both audio stems for "Dorito" copied from the user-provided mod folder "TO JEST TEN MOD". Inst.ogg and Voices.ogg play together as one song. No character sprites are included.

The four transparent arrow images are cropped from Psych Engine's NOTE_assets.png; the Sick/Good/Bad/Shit rating images are also from the local Psych Engine download. Its bundled readme credits ninjamuffin99, PhantomArcade, kawaisprite, and evilsk8r and states that the game's art may be reused. These credits and the mod name are documented here for attribution.

The game code adapts this mod's chart data for a small browser rhythm game. It supports WASD and arrow keys, uses the audio playback clock to keep notes in sync, restores the chat when the song ends, and posts score, misses, and weighted accuracy as a MądraAI reply.
